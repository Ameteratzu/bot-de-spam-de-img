# Telegram iOS interface icons

The PNG icons in this directory are copied or rendered from Telegram's official iOS source repository:

- Attachment and microphone: `submodules/TelegramUI/Images.xcassets/Chat/Input/Text/`
- Schedule clock: `submodules/TelegramUI/Images.xcassets/Chat/Input/Text/AccessoryIconSchedule.imageset/`
- Back and read receipts: rendered from `submodules/TelegramUI/Images.xcassets/Navigation/Back.imageset/` and `submodules/TelegramUI/Images.xcassets/Chat/Context Menu/Read.imageset/`

Source: [TelegramMessenger/Telegram-iOS](https://github.com/TelegramMessenger/Telegram-iOS).
